Ballpark Passport Support

Ballpark Passport keeps a record of the ballparks and games you've been to.

Get help

Open a support request on GitHub. Say which iPhone and iOS version you have and what happened. Please don't include personal details: support requests there are public.

Common questions

Where is my passport stored? On your iPhone, and in your private iCloud if iCloud is on for Ballpark Passport. We don't keep a copy.

My games aren't on my other iPhone or iPad. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account on both, and that iCloud is on for Ballpark Passport in Settings > [your name] > iCloud. Syncing can take a few minutes.

"Find Games in My Photos" didn't find a game. It looks for photos with a location inside a ballpark on a game day. Photos without location data, or shared from someone else, can't be matched; add those games by hand.

How do I restore Ballpark Passport Pro? Pro is tied to your Apple Account. Open the Pro screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I cancel my subscription? Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > Ballpark Passport. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.

Scores or schedules aren't loading. Check your connection and try again in a minute. If it keeps happening, open a support request.

How do I delete my data? Delete games in the app, then delete the app. Data in iCloud can be removed in Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage.
