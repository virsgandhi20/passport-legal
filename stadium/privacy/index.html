Ballpark Passport Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Ballpark Passport keeps a record of the ballparks and games you go to. This policy explains what the app handles and where it goes. The short version: your passport stays private, on your iPhone and in your iCloud. We have no servers that store your data, we don't track you, and there are no ads.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your games and passport: the games you log, scores, seats, notes, companions, stamps and stats are stored on your device.
  • Your profile: the name and favourite team you enter, and app settings, are stored on your device.
  • Sign in with Apple: if you sign in, the app stores the anonymous user identifier Apple gives it (and your name, if you share it) on your device. We do not run an account server, so this is not sent to us.
  • Notification tokens: the device and Live Activity tokens Apple issues are kept on your device. They are not sent anywhere today.

iCloud

If you're signed in to iCloud, your games sync between your devices through your private iCloud database (CloudKit). Apple stores this data under your Apple Account. We cannot see it. You can turn iCloud off for the app in Settings.

Photos

If you choose "Find Games in My Photos", the app reads the location and date of photos in your library to find ones taken inside a ballpark. This happens entirely on your iPhone. Your photos and their locations are never uploaded. You can change Photos access at any time in Settings.

Services the app uses

  • Apple Weather (WeatherKit): to show the forecast for a game, the app sends the ballpark's location and the game time to Apple. Your own location is not used.
  • Apple Maps (MapKit): maps, 3D views and nearby parking are loaded from Apple using ballpark locations.
  • Scores and schedules: to look up games, the app asks our data proxy (a small server function hosted by Supabase) for schedules and scores by date, team or game. The proxy fetches them from a third-party sports data service (BALLDONTLIE) and passes back only the game data. The requests contain no personal information and no identifier for you or your device. Like any internet request, they reveal your IP address to the proxy; it is used only in memory to limit how often one address can call, and is not stored or logged by us. The sports data service sees only the proxy, never you. Supabase may keep standard technical request logs for a limited time to run its service.
  • In-app purchases: Ballpark Passport Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we only receive confirmation of what you're entitled to.
  • Notifications and Live Activities: game reminders are scheduled on your device. Live Activities show the score on your Lock Screen.

What we don't do

  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No ads, no analytics SDKs, no selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No account server: we don't hold a copy of your passport.

Deleting your data

Delete a game in the app to remove it from your device and your iCloud. Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device; data in your private iCloud can be removed in Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage. Sign out of Sign in with Apple from the app's Profile, and stop using it with the app in Settings > [your name] > Sign in with Apple.

Children

Ballpark Passport is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date. If a future version adds features that send data to us (for example, friends), the policy will be updated before that feature ships.
