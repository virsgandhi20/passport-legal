Paddock Passport Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Paddock Passport is built to keep your passport private. There is no Paddock Passport account. The developer does not collect, sell or share your personal data.

What stays on your iPhone

  • The races you log (which weekends you went to or are going to, your seat and your notes), your medals and stamps.
  • Your settings: favourite team, sounds and haptics, map style, medal finish, app icon.
  • Your profile photo and name, if you add them.
  • Copies of the race calendar, results, standings and timing, cached so the app works offline.

What goes to your iCloud

If you're signed in to iCloud, your races are stored in your own private iCloud database (Apple's CloudKit private database) so they appear on your other devices. Your profile name and photo sync the same way, through iCloud key-value storage. Only you can see this data. The developer can't read it. You can turn this off for Paddock Passport in Settings > [your name] > iCloud.

Sign in with Apple

Signing in is optional. If you sign in, Apple shares an anonymous user identifier and, the first time only, your name. The identifier is kept on your iPhone so the app can tell whether you're still signed in; your name is kept with your profile as described above. Nothing is sent to the developer. You can sign out in Profile at any time, or stop using Sign in with Apple with Paddock Passport in Settings > [your name] > Sign-In & Security.

Apple services

  • Apple Weather (WeatherKit): to show race-day forecasts, the app asks Apple Weather for the forecast at the circuit's location. Your own location is never used.
  • Apple Maps (MapKit): maps, satellite imagery and Look Around are provided by Apple.
  • Calendar: if you choose "Add to Calendar", the app adds sessions to your calendar. It has write-only access and never reads your calendar.
  • Notifications: session reminders are scheduled on your iPhone.
  • In-app purchases: handled by the App Store. The developer receives no payment details.

Apple's handling of this data is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

Race data

The app downloads the race calendar, results, standings, live timing and lap times from Sportmonks, a licensed motorsport data provider, through a small server the developer runs on Supabase. The app's requests carry no personal data and no identifier for you or your device. Like any web request, a request reveals your device's IP address to that server; the server uses it only briefly, in memory, to limit how many requests one address can make, and does not store it. The server passes no information about you to Sportmonks.

No tracking, no ads

Paddock Passport has no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Paddock Passport is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Remove races in the app (open a race and choose Remove from Passport, or Profile > Remove All Races). Deleting the app removes everything stored on your iPhone. Data in your iCloud can be deleted in Settings > [your name] > iCloud
