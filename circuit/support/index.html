Circuit Passport Support

Circuit Passport keeps every grand prix you've been to, and helps you find your seat at the next one.

Get help

Open a support request on GitHub. Say which iPhone and iOS version you have and what happened. Please don't include personal details: support requests there are public.

Common questions

Where is my passport stored? On your iPhone, and in your private iCloud if iCloud is on for Circuit Passport. There is no Circuit Passport server.

My races aren't on my other iPhone or iPad. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account on both, and that iCloud is on for Circuit Passport in Settings > [your name] > iCloud. Syncing can take a few minutes.

How do I restore Circuit Passport Pro? Pro is tied to your Apple Account. Open the Pro screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I cancel my subscription? Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > Circuit Passport. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.

The weather forecast is missing. Forecasts appear up to 10 days before a session. Earlier than that, or without a connection, the forecast row is hidden.

Session reminders didn't arrive. Check Settings > Notifications > Circuit Passport, and that you've marked the race as "I'm going."

A session time, grandstand or corner looks wrong. Calendar data comes from public sources and seat details are approximate. Open a support request with the circuit and what looks wrong.

How do I delete my data? Remove races in the app (Profile > Remove All Races), then delete the app. Data in iCloud can be removed in Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage.
